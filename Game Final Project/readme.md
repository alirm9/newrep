# The Sustainable Herder - Text Adventure Game

## Game Idea
"The Sustainable Herder" is a command-line text adventure game where the player takes on the role of a farmer who has inherited a small herd of animals and a damaged pasture. The game focuses on resource management and environmental responsibility.

## Objective
The player's goal is to survive for 10 months while balancing three critical resources:
- **Money:** Required to purchase food and maintain the farm.
- **Animal Health:** Determines the monthly income generated by the herd.
- **Pasture Quality:** Represents the ecological health of the land.

The ultimate objective is to achieve the "Sustainable Herder" ending by keeping all three resources at healthy levels, avoiding both bankruptcy and environmental destruction.

## Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Integration
This game incorporates **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production)** and **SDG 15 (Life on Land)**. 
Throughout the game, players face dilemmas that test their commitment to sustainability. For instance, choosing "Free Grazing" costs nothing but leads to overgrazing and land degradation (desertification). Conversely, "Rotational Grazing" requires financial investment but allows the ecosystem to recover. The game actively demonstrates that prioritizing short-term profit at the expense of nature leads to a negative outcome (The Greedy Farmer ending).

## Operating Principles and Functionalities
- **Core Loop:** The game runs on a `while` loop that iterates through 10 in-game months.
- **Menu System:** Players interact with the game using a numbered command-line interface.
- **Resource Limits:** Variables are constrained (e.g., health and pasture quality cannot exceed 100% or drop below 0%).
- **Dynamic Events:** A random event system introduces unpredictability (e.g., weather changes or predator attacks) once per month.
- **Multiple Routes/Endings:** The game tracks the player's decisions and calculates one of 4 possible endings based on the final status of the player's finances and environment.
